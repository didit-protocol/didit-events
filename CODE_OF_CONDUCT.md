# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment and personal attacks are
not accepted in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately by email to [security@didit.me](mailto:security@didit.me), with "Conduct" at the start
of the subject. Access to that mailbox is restricted to the people at Didit who are authorized to handle reports.

Say what happened, where (a link helps) and who was involved. Do not report conduct problems in a public issue.

## What happens next

1. A maintainer acknowledges the report within five working days.
2. Maintainers who are not involved in the incident review it. Someone named in a report takes no part in handling it.
3. The reporter is told the outcome. Their identity is not shared with the person reported without their consent.

Possible outcomes follow the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant: a private correction, a warning, a
temporary ban from the project spaces, or a permanent ban. Maintainers may also edit or remove comments, commits and
other contributions that break this code.
